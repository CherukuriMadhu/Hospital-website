About Our Hospital

Dedicated to providing world-class healthcare with a human touch. Serving the people of Kodad and surrounding areas since 2021.

Our Journey

A Legacy of Care and Trust

Swathi Hospital was established in 2021 with a simple mission: to make high-quality, specialized healthcare accessible to the residents of Kodad and Suryapet district. Located conveniently on Huzur Nagar Road, we have quickly become the preferred choice for multi-specialty care in the region.

Our hospital is equipped with 50+ beds, advanced diagnostic facilities, and a dedicated team of specialists in Gynaecology, Surgery, and General Medicine. We operate 24/7, ensuring that medical help is always available when you need it most.

"We believe that healthcare is a fundamental right. Our goal is to provide treatment that is not only effective but also compassionate and affordable."
